AIDE is made by Marshall Ku. This policy covers the AIDE apps for iPhone, Mac and Linux and the services
the developer runs for them. It was last updated on October 25, 2026.

## The short version

- There's no AIDE account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.
- Your notes, todos and settings stay on your device, on a computer that hosts your vault, or on a server
  you run. The developer never receives them.
- What leaves your devices goes where you set it up: your own server, or the AI provider you choose.

## What the developer receives

- **Notifications to an iPhone.** A server without its own Apple push key sends iPhone notifications
  through the developer's push relay. The relay passes each notification's title and body to Apple and
  doesn't store them. When a delivery fails, its log keeps a short hash of the device's notification token
  (not the token itself), so a broken device can be told apart. Nothing in it names you.
- **Nothing else.** The apps don't report usage, crashes or errors to the developer. (If you turned on
  **Share With App Developers** in your iPhone's or Mac's settings, Apple passes crash reports on.)

## What goes to services you set up

- **Your server or host.** In server mode, the app sends your notes, todos and settings to the server you
  connected to, over TLS. That server is yours, or run by whoever you chose.
- **The AI you set up.** AI runs only where you set it up: an Anthropic API key in the app, Claude Code or
  Codex on your computer, your own gateway, or your server's AI. Then the assistant, Organize and the other
  AI features send what they need (the messages you write, your open todos, today's events, the notes the
  assistant reads) to that provider, under the account it uses. The provider keeps them according to its
  own policy and that account's settings. With no AI set up, nothing goes to an AI provider.
  [Privacy and security](/docs/self-hosting/privacy/) lists every feature and what it sends.
- **GitHub.** The app reads the plugin index and plugin files from GitHub Pages, and AIDE from this website
  on a Mac or Linux computer checks for updates on GitHub, the way a browser loads a page. No personal
  data is sent.
- **Apple.** On the iPhone and Mac, speech you dictate is turned into text on the device, and the Calendar
  reads the calendars already on it.

## What stays on your device

Voice and meeting audio, your device's calendars, a device plugin's own storage, and, without a server,
all of your notes. See [Privacy and security](/docs/self-hosting/privacy/) for the details.

## Children

AIDE isn't directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

## Your choices

You can use AIDE with no server and no AI. You can delete your notes, a device, or the whole vault from
the app at any time. Removing the app removes what it kept in its own storage; a folder you picked for
your notes stays where it is.
