The Eisenhower matrix shows all your open todos in four boxes, by how important and how urgent they
are, so you can see what to do first.

<PluginFacts id="eisenhower" kind="device" />

## What it does

It adds a **Matrix** tab with four boxes:

| Box          | Holds                       |
| ------------ | --------------------------- |
| **Do**       | Urgent · important          |
| **Schedule** | Important, not urgent       |
| **Delegate** | Urgent, not important       |
| **Later**    | Neither                     |

- **Important** means the todo's priority is high or highest (`⏫` or `🔺`, or `[priority:: high]`).
- **Urgent** means it's due or scheduled within two days, or overdue (`📅 2026-10-22`, `⏳ …`).

Each todo shows its date. Tap the button in front of one to check it off; the todo's line in its note
is marked done, and nothing else in the note changes. The matrix reloads each time you come back to
the tab. The writing of priorities and dates is in [Todos](/docs/guides/todos/).

## Set it up

<Steps>

1. Open **Browse community plugins** (in **Settings**, under **Community plugins**; on Linux, the **Community
   plugins** page of Settings) and choose **Eisenhower matrix**.
2. Approve **Read your todos, open and done, in any note** and **Check off and add todos, in any
   note**, then **Install**.
3. Open the **Matrix** tab. To hide the tab without removing the plugin, turn off **Show as tab** in
   its settings.

</Steps>

It has no settings. More on installing, updating and removing is in [Installing device
plugins](/docs/plugins/device-plugins/).

## Privacy

The plugin reads your todos (their text and fields, not the rest of your notes) and changes only the
lines of todos you check off. It makes no network requests and keeps no data of its own.
