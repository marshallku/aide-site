Recall helps you remember what you write. You review cards on a forgetting curve in a **Recall** tab,
every night your AI turns what you newly wrote into cards for you to accept or discard, and every
morning you're told how many cards are due.

Recall has two halves with the same name. The **device plugin** is the review screen; it works on its
own, on any device. The **server plugin** makes cards overnight and sends the reminder; it needs a
server or a [desktop host](/docs/self-hosting/desktop-host/).

## What it does

### Cards you write

Write cards in any note under a card folder (`cards` by default, so `cards/biology`,
`cards/spanish/verbs`…). There are three forms.

A line with `::` between the front and the back. A list marker in front is fine:

```markdown
photosynthesis :: how plants make food from light
- mitochondria :: where a cell makes most of its energy
```

A line with `:::` makes two cards, one in each direction:

```markdown
der Hund ::: the dog
```

A block whose front and back are separated by a line holding only `?`. Use `??` for both directions:

```markdown
What are the three stages of cellular respiration?
?
Glycolysis, the Krebs cycle,
and the electron transport chain.
```

A blank line or a heading ends a block. Frontmatter, fenced code, HTML comments, todo lines and lines
of only tags are never cards. To write `::` in a card without splitting it, put it in a code span or
write `\::`.

### Reviewing

The **Recall** tab shows how many cards are **Due now**, how many are **New today** and how many are
**Not yet seen**. Choose **Start review**:

- Each card shows its front. **Show answer** (or <kbd>Space</kbd>) shows the back.
- Grade it **Again**, **Hard**, **Good** or **Easy** (<kbd>1</kbd> to <kbd>4</kbd>). Each grade shows
  when you'll see the card next.
- **Again** brings the card back in 10 minutes. Any other grade schedules it days to years ahead,
  using FSRS-5 aimed at 90% retention.
- The most overdue card comes first. Once nothing is due, new cards come, up to **New cards a day**
  (20) across all your devices.

Each review is one line in a note of this device's own, `recall/log/<device>/<month>`. Every device
reads all the logs, so your iPhone and your Mac agree on what's due, and no two devices ever edit the
same note.

### Cards made from your notes

At 22:30 the server plugin gathers the paragraphs you wrote since the last run in `topics` and
`daily`, and asks your AI for cards. It leaves out frontmatter, code, blocks that plugins wrote (such
as the briefing), todos and anything that is already a card. It writes at most 10 cards a night into
`recall/cards/<date>`, each marked as waiting.

- Each passage is sent once. A long one goes in pieces of 4 KB; what doesn't fit tonight waits for
  the next night, oldest first.
- On the first night, only notes changed in the last 24 hours count.
- If the run is retried, the AI isn't asked twice for the same night.

The cards wait in the **Recall** tab under **Made from your notes**. Choose **Accept** or **Discard**
on each, or **Accept all**. An accepted card joins your reviews; a discarded one is deleted from the
note. Nothing the AI writes becomes a card until you accept it.

### The morning reminder

At 08:00 the server plugin counts the cards due by the end of the day in your card folders and in
`recall/cards`, from every device's review log, plus the new cards and those waiting in the inbox. It
posts one Feed item, "N cards to review today", with what's waiting, and pushes it to your devices.
When there's nothing at all, it posts nothing.

## Before you start

- **For reviews:** AIDE on an iPhone, Mac or Linux computer. Nothing else.
- **For cards made from your notes and the reminder:** a server or a desktop host running the
  server plugins, and an AI set up for it ([AI and models](/docs/guides/ai/)).

## Set it up

<Steps>

1. Install the device plugin: open **Browse community plugins** (in **Settings**, under **Community plugins**;
   on Linux, the **Community plugins** page of Settings) and choose **Recall**. Approve **Read and search all
   notes** and **Read, change and archive notes in recall**, then **Install**. Details are in
   [Installing device plugins](/docs/plugins/device-plugins/).
2. Write a few cards in a note under `cards/`, then open the **Recall** tab.
3. On a server or desktop host, open **Settings** and choose **Recall** under **Core plugins**
   (on Linux, the **Recall** row on the **Core plugins** page of Settings). Turn on **Enabled**. The plugin starts within a
   minute.
4. Install the device plugin on each device you review on. The review log keeps them in step.

</Steps>

### Server plugin

<PluginSettings

    defaults={{
        at: "08:00",
        prefixes: "cards",
        push: "force",
        sources: "topics, daily",
        generateAt: "22:30",
        maxCards: "10",
    }}
/>

<Aside>
Keep the server plugin's **Card folders** the same as the device plugin's. Otherwise the reminder
counts different cards from the ones you review.
</Aside>

- **Notification**: `force` always pushes the reminder, `never` only puts it in the Feed, and the
  empty choice (**Default**) lets the Feed's triage decide.
- **Cards a night at most**: up to 50. Set it to `0` to stop making cards while keeping the reminder.
- A missed run (the host asleep) still happens if the computer is back within 3 hours of its time.

## Troubleshooting

### The Recall tab says No cards yet

Cards are read only from notes under the card folders. Check the note's path starts with `cards/` (or
your own folder), and that each card is on one line with `::`, or a block with a `?` line between
front and back.

### No cards came from my notes

- Check the server plugin is **Enabled** and that an AI is set up.
- Only paragraphs under `topics` and `daily` (or your **Make cards from** folders) written since the
  last run count. Todos, code and plugin-written blocks are skipped.
- **Cards a night at most** set to `0` turns card making off.

### The reminder didn't arrive

It's posted only when something is due, new or waiting. With **Notification** set to `never` it's
in the Feed but not pushed.

### Some reviews look missing

The tab warns when a review log line can't be read, or when there are more notes or logs than it
reads at once. Logs are kept per month; a month's log can fill up, and the tab says so.

## Privacy

- Your cards and review logs are notes in your vault, synced like any other note.
- The device plugin reads notes and writes only under `recall/`. It makes no network requests.
- Card making sends the new passages from your source folders to the AI you set up (a provider such
  as Anthropic, or your own CLI). Each passage is sent once.
- The reminder is a Feed item with the day's counts.
