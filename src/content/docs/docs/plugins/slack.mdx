The Slack plugin turns a Slack conversation worth keeping into a note. Choose **Save to AIDE** on any
message and its whole thread lands in your vault, with a summary, the decisions and the action items
on top. With **Follow my channels** on, it also keeps your channels' messages on your computer for
[Radar](/docs/plugins/radar/).

<PluginFacts id="slack" />

## What it does

### Save to AIDE

In Slack, open a message's **⋮** (More actions) menu and choose **Save to AIDE**. It works on the
thread's first message or on any reply, in public and private channels, DMs and group DMs.

The plugin reads the whole thread, every page of it, and writes it into one note per thread:

```text
inbox/slack/<channel>/<date>-<first line>
```

The date is the day the thread started; the name comes from its first line. DMs go under
`inbox/slack/dm/`. A few seconds later Slack answers you, privately, with `Saved to AIDE: <path>`, and
a **Saved from Slack** item appears in the Feed (not pushed).

A saved note looks like this:

```markdown
---
source: slack
slack: T0123/C0456/1729500000.000100
channel: "#launch"
permalink: https://your-team.slack.com/archives/C0456/p1729500000000100
saved: 2026-10-21
tags: [slack]
---
# Can we move the launch to Thursday?

<!-- aide:slot slack.summary -->
_Summary, 2026-10-21 14:05_

The team agreed to move the launch to Thursday so QA can finish the payment tests…

**Decisions**
- Launch on Thursday at 10:00

**Action items**
- [ ] Tell support about the new date (Mina)
<!-- /aide:slot slack.summary -->

<!-- aide:slot slack.thread -->
…every message, with its author and time…
<!-- /aide:slot slack.thread -->
```

- **The summary** comes from your AI: a few sentences, the decisions, and the action items as todos,
  which show up with your other todos. Turn off **Summarize** to save threads without it.
- **The thread** is each message's author and time, with its text quoted. Slack's formatting becomes
  Markdown, mentions become names, and files become links.
- **Saving the same thread again** updates the same note. Only the two sections are rewritten; what
  you added around them stays. The summary is asked for again only when the thread changed, so the
  action items you ticked stay ticked.

### Who can save

Only you can save: the person whose user token the plugin has, in that workspace. Add other people's
Slack member ids to **Also allowed** to let them save into your AIDE too. Anyone else who uses the
shortcut is told `Only the owner of this AIDE can save to it.` and nothing is saved.

### When a save fails

A save is kept by the plugin before Slack is answered, so a crash or a restart loses nothing. A save
that fails is tried again a minute later, up to 6 times. If it still fails, or Slack refuses for good
(no access to the channel, the thread is gone), Slack tells you
`AIDE couldn't save this thread: <reason>` and the Feed gets **A Slack thread wasn't saved**.

### Follow my channels

With **Follow my channels** on, every message said in the channels you're in, with its edits and
deletions, goes into a feed kept **on this computer only**, for 14 days. Nothing of it is sent to the
AIDE server. [Radar](/docs/plugins/radar/) reads this feed to tell you what you'd miss.

- **Only these channels** limits it to the channels you name; empty means every channel you're in.
- **Never these channels** leaves channels out.
- **Follow DMs** includes direct and group messages (on by default).
- Joins, topic changes and link previews are left out.

When the plugin was disconnected for more than 2 minutes (the computer asleep, AIDE closed), it reads
back what it missed, at most the last 24 hours: each followed conversation's new messages, the
replies to their threads, and the replies to the threads active in the last 3 days (up to 200). What
it couldn't check is noted in the feed, so Radar knows.

## Before you start

- **A desktop host or a server running the server plugins.** The plugin keeps a connection to Slack
  open from the computer it runs on. Slack can reach it only while that computer is awake and the host
  is running ([Host on your desktop](/docs/self-hosting/desktop-host/)).
- **A Slack workspace where you may install apps.** Some workspaces need an admin's approval first.
- **An AI** for summaries ([AI and models](/docs/guides/ai/)). Without one, threads are saved without
  a summary.

The plugin uses **Socket Mode**: it connects out to Slack, so nothing on the internet has to reach
your computer, and it works behind a home router or a company VPN. It reads Slack **as you**, with a
user token, so it sees exactly what you see, private channels and DMs included, with no bot to invite.

## Set it up

You create your own Slack app (it takes a few minutes), then give the plugin its two tokens.

<Steps>

1. In AIDE, open **Settings** and choose **Slack** under **On the server** (on Linux, **Settings ›
   Plugins › Slack**). The status under its settings says **Add the app token and user token**.
2. Next to **App token**, choose **Create the Slack app**. Slack's **Create an app** page opens with
   the app's manifest filled in: the **Save to AIDE** shortcut, the permissions it reads with, the
   message events for following, and Socket Mode. Pick your workspace, check the summary and choose
   **Create**.
3. In the app's settings on Slack, open **Install App** and choose **Install to &lt;your
   workspace&gt;**, then **Allow**.
4. Open **OAuth & Permissions** and copy the **User OAuth Token**. It starts with `xoxp-`.
5. Back in AIDE, paste it into **User token** and choose **Save**.
6. On Slack, open **Basic Information**, scroll to **App-Level Tokens** and choose **Generate Token
   and Scopes**. Name it (for example `aide`), choose **Add Scope**, pick `connections:write`, and
   choose **Generate**. Copy the token; it starts with `xapp-`.
7. In AIDE, paste it into **App token** and choose **Save**.
8. Turn on **Enabled**. Within a minute the status says **Connected as &lt;you&gt; in &lt;your
   workspace&gt;**.
9. In Slack, open any message's **⋮** menu and choose **Save to AIDE**. The first time, it may be under
   **More message shortcuts…**.

</Steps>

The tokens are secret settings. AIDE seals them on the server, only the Slack plugin can read them,
and the app never shows them again: a saved token reads **Saved in AIDE**, with **Replace** and
**Remove**.

<Aside type="caution">
Connect one Slack app to one AIDE. Slack hands a Socket Mode app's shortcuts to any of its
connections, so two AIDEs sharing an app would each get some of your saves. For a second AIDE, create
a second app.
</Aside>

To follow your channels for Radar, also turn on **Follow my channels**.

- **Save into**: each channel gets its own folder inside, named after the channel; DMs go into `dm`.
  Clearing it brings back `inbox/slack`.
- **Also allowed**: member ids look like `U01AB2CD3EF`. In Slack, open someone's profile, choose
  **⋮** and **Copy member ID**.
- **Only these channels** and **Never these channels** take channel names, as Slack shows them.

Changing a setting restarts the plugin; it picks the change up within a minute.

## Status

The line under the plugin's settings says how it's doing. It updates every few seconds.

| Status                                  | What it means                                                                    |
| --------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Connected as … in …**                 | Connected to Slack as you, in that workspace. Save to AIDE works.                |
| **Add the app token and user token**    | One or both tokens are missing. The plugin waits until both are saved.           |
| **Slack refused the tokens: `<code>`**  | Slack won't accept them. The plugin waits until you change them (see below).     |
| **Reconnecting to Slack…**              | The connection dropped; the plugin is connecting again by itself.                |

## Troubleshooting

### Slack refused the tokens

The code says why:

- `invalid_auth` or `not_authed`: a token was pasted wrong, or the two tokens are from different
  apps. Copy both again from the same app.
- `token_revoked`: the app was uninstalled or the token regenerated. Install the app again and save
  the new **User OAuth Token**.
- `missing_scope`: the app lacks a permission, usually because it wasn't made with **Create the Slack
  app**. Create a new app that way.
- `account_inactive`: the account the token belongs to was deactivated.

### Save to AIDE isn't in the menu

The app isn't installed in this workspace, or Slack hasn't loaded it yet. Check **Install App** in the
app's settings, then look under **More message shortcuts…** in the message's **⋮** menu.

### The shortcut does nothing, or Slack reports an error

Slack can reach the plugin only while it's connected. If the computer running it is asleep or off,
the host isn't running, or the plugin is off, the save can't be taken. Check that the status in AIDE
says **Connected as … in …**, then save again.

### Only the owner of this AIDE can save to it

You used the shortcut from an account other than the token's, or in another workspace. Add your
member id to **Also allowed**, or use the account the **User token** belongs to.

### AIDE couldn't save this thread

The reason follows. `channel_not_found` or `not_in_channel` means the token's account can't read that
conversation. `thread_too_long` means Slack won't return the whole thread. Other reasons (network,
the server) were retried 6 times; try again later.

### Radar sees no Slack messages

- Turn on **Follow my channels**, on the same computer Radar runs on.
- The app needs the message events. An app made with **Create the Slack app** has them. For an app
  made another way, add the user events `message.channels`, `message.groups`, `message.im` and
  `message.mpim` under **Event Subscriptions**, then reinstall the app. Without them only the shortcut
  works.
- Radar's status note, `radar/status`, says when Slack last said something on this computer.

## Privacy

- The plugin connects from your computer to Slack. Nothing connects in.
- It reads Slack with your user token, so it can read what your account can. It doesn't post, except
  its private replies to your own shortcut.
- A saved thread becomes a note, stored and synced like any other note. If **Summarize** is on, the
  thread's text goes to the AI you set up.
- With **Follow my channels**, your channels' messages are kept in a feed on this computer for 14
  days and read only by Radar. They never go to the AIDE server.
- The tokens are sealed on the server, readable only by this plugin, and never shown in the apps.
