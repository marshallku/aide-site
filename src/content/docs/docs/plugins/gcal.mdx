The Google Calendar plugin brings your Google calendars into AIDE: they show in the Calendar section, today's events go into your daily note, and a few minutes before each meeting you get a reminder and notes on what to prepare.

<PluginFacts id="gcal" />

## What it does

**Keeps your calendars in AIDE.** Every 5 minutes it reads your calendar list and the events from 7 days ago to 6 weeks ahead (at most 300 events per calendar), and the [Calendar](/docs/guides/calendar/) section shows them. If one calendar can't be read, its last good events stay and the calendar shows **Sync failed** with the reason.

**Writes today's agenda.** In a daily note made from a template with `{{slot gcal.today}}`, it lists the day's events from the calendars you show, all-day events first:

```markdown
- All day Team offsite
- 09:30–10:00 Standup (Room 4)
- 14:00–15:00 Design review
```

A day with nothing reads `- No events`. The list is written when the daily is created and kept current on every sync, so an event added or moved during the day shows up within 5 minutes.

**Reminds you before events.** Every minute it checks the calendars that have reminders on. When a timed event starts within your reminder time (10 minutes by default), it posts a reminder to the Feed, titled like "In 10 minutes: Design review", with the time, the place, a link to the event and its video-call link. Reminders always notify you. All-day events get no reminder. Each event is reminded once.

**Writes prep notes.** Right after a reminder, it asks your AI what to prepare: the AI gets the event and the notes that match its title, and answers with what the meeting is about, related open todos or decisions, and one to three things to do. The answer goes into the day note (`briefings/{date}`) under a heading like `#### 14:00 Prep for Design review`. With **Tell me when there's something to prepare** on, the AI also decides whether it found something concrete (a document to read, a pending decision, something to bring), and only then sends it to you as a notification, "14:00 Prep for Design review". Prep runs one event at a time, up to 90 seconds each; when more than 8 are waiting, the extra ones are skipped.

**Fills in meeting notes.** In a [meeting note](/docs/guides/meetings/) made from one of its events, it keeps a section with what the calendar knows: when, where, the video link, the other attendees, the event link and its description, and marks it cancelled if the event is.

<Badge text="Next release" /> An event without a title is called "(No title)" in the agenda, reminders, prep notes and the app's calendar, in the language plugins write in.

## Before you start

- A [desktop host](/docs/self-hosting/desktop-host/) or your own server. The plugin runs there and reads Google from that machine.
- A Google account. <Badge text="Next release" /> You sign in from the plugin's settings in the app; you don't need an OAuth client of your own. Until this release, the plugin needed `credentials.json` and a token file you made yourself (see [Your own OAuth files](#your-own-oauth-files)).
- For prep notes: an AI set in **Settings › AI** (**Anthropic API key** or **My gateway**).

## Set it up

<Steps>

1. Turn the plugin on: **Calendar** under **Settings › Core plugins**, then **Enabled** (on Linux, the switch on its row on the **Core plugins** page of Settings). See [Turn a plugin on](/docs/plugins/overview/#turn-a-plugin-on).
2. <Badge text="Next release" /> Within a minute the plugin's status, under its settings, reads "Sign in to read your calendars…" with a **Sign in with Google** button. Choose it, pick your Google account and allow AIDE to see your calendars.
    - **On the computer that runs the plugins** (the desktop host's own app): the browser comes back to AIDE by itself and says "AIDE is signed in to Google. You can close this tab."
    - **From a phone or another computer**: the browser ends on a page that doesn't load, at an address starting with `http://127.0.0.1`. Copy that whole address, paste it into the plugin's **Sign-in link** setting and save. The plugin picks it up within a few seconds.

    The status then reads "Connected to Google Calendar". A sign-in link works once, for 10 minutes; after that the button opens a new one.
3. Within a minute or two your calendars appear in the Calendar section.
4. Choose **Calendars…** under the plugin to pick which calendars show and remind you, and their colors.
5. For the agenda in your daily, add `{{slot gcal.today}}` to your daily template (see [Daily notes and templates](/docs/guides/periodic-notes/)).

</Steps>

<Aside type="caution" title="Google may warn that it hasn't verified AIDE">
Until Google finishes reviewing AIDE's sign-in, the consent page can say **Google hasn't verified this app**. To go on, choose **Advanced**, then **Go to AIDE (unsafe)**. AIDE asks only to read, and the token stays on the computer that runs the plugins. While the review is pending, Google also limits how many people can sign in with AIDE's client; if it turns you away, use [your own OAuth files](#your-own-oauth-files).
</Aside>

The token stays in the plugin's state folder on the computer that runs the plugins. The [morning briefing](/docs/plugins/briefing/) reads your calendar through this same sign-in.

## Settings

<PluginSettings id="gcal" defaults={{ signInLink: "" }} />

**Sign-in link** <Badge text="Next release" /> is only for signing in from a phone or another computer (see [Set it up](#set-it-up)).

The rest of the plugin's choices are in the **Calendars…** sheet, under the plugin in Settings (on Linux, the **Calendars…** row in its expanded row):

| In the sheet                                         | What it does                                                                                      | Default |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------- |
| Each calendar's switch                               | Shows the calendar in AIDE and in the daily agenda                                                 | What Google's own calendar list shows |
| Each calendar's bell                                 | Reminders for that calendar's events                                                               | On for every calendar |
| Each calendar's color dot                            | Its color in AIDE: Google's palette, a custom color, or **Use Google's color**                     | Google's color |
| **Remind before events**                             | Turns reminders on or off for all calendars                                                        | On |
| **When**                                             | How long before: 5, 10, 15, 30 or 60 **min before**                                                | 10 min before |
| **AI prep notes in the daily note**                  | Writes prep notes after each reminder                                                              | On |
| **Tell me when there's something to prepare**        | Also notifies you when the AI finds something concrete to prepare                                  | Off |

Calendars are grouped as **My calendars** and **Other calendars**, like Google Calendar.

### Local keys

In `plugins.json` on a desktop host (see [Local settings](/docs/plugins/overview/#local-settings)), under `"gcal"`:

| Key               | What it does                                                                                             |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `credentials`     | Path to your own OAuth client file (see below). Empty: AIDE's.                                           |
| `tokenFile`       | Path to your own token file (see below). Empty: the account you signed in with from the app.             |
| `note`            | Where prep notes go: a note path with `{date}`, or `daily` for the daily note. Default `briefings/{date}`. |
| `reminderMinutes` | Reminder time used until you choose one in the sheet. Default `10`.                                      |
| `prep`            | Prep notes on or off until you choose in the sheet. Default `true`.                                      |
| `prepNotify`      | **Tell me…** on or off until you choose in the sheet. Default `false`.                                   |

```json
{
    "plugins": {
        "gcal": {
            "credentials": "~/secrets/google/credentials.json",
            "tokenFile": "~/secrets/google/calendar-token.json",
            "note": "daily"
        }
    }
}
```

### Your own OAuth files

Setups from before Sign in with Google keep working. The plugin uses, in this order:

1. The token file named by `tokenFile`, with the client named by `credentials`.
2. <Badge text="Next release" /> The account you signed in with from the app.
3. The files it read before this release, when they exist: `~/.config/life-assistant/google-token.json`, with the client at `~/.config/life-assistant/credentials.json`.

A token file must be granted the read-only calendar scope (`https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly`) for that same client. When Google refreshes it, the plugin saves the new one in its own state folder and never writes into your token file. If you replace your token file later (after signing in again), the newer file wins. While one of these files is in use, the plugin doesn't offer **Sign in with Google**, unless Google refuses the old files' token (see [Calendar asks me to sign in again](#calendar-asks-me-to-sign-in-again)).

## Troubleshooting

### No calendars show up

- <Badge text="Next release" /> Look at the plugin's status under its settings. "Sign in to read your calendars…" means it's still waiting for you to sign in.
- "That sign-in link is old or already used; sign in again": the pasted address came from an earlier attempt. Choose **Sign in with Google** again and paste the new address.
- "This build can't sign in to Google; set credentials in the plugins' config file": this copy of AIDE has no Google client built in. Use [your own OAuth files](#your-own-oauth-files).
- With your own files, the plugin can't start until it can read them. It tries again every 30 seconds, and the host log (its path is under **Log** in Settings › Host) says which file it couldn't read. Check the paths, and that the token was issued for the same OAuth client as `credentials.json`.

### Calendar asks me to sign in again

<Badge text="Next release" /> Google stopped taking the plugin's access (you removed it at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions) or changed your password). The plugin sets the refused token aside and shows **Sign in with Google** again. That holds for the app's sign-in and for the files it read before this release (`~/.config/life-assistant/google-token.json`): such a file is skipped until it changes, and never deleted. A token file you named with `tokenFile` isn't set aside; sign in again with your own tooling and replace the file.

### google: no refresh token came back

Google didn't give a lasting token, usually because AIDE was allowed before. Remove AIDE's access at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions) and sign in again.

### Prep notes aren't in my daily note

They go into the day note, `briefings/{date}`, unless `note` in `plugins.json` says `daily`. Prep also needs an AI in **Settings › AI**.

### A reminder never came for an event during sleep

A reminder that came due while the computer slept is skipped on waking: it would arrive after the event started.

### The daily agenda says "Some calendars could not be read"

One calendar failed for that day while the others worked. It's usually a calendar you lost access to; turn its switch off in **Calendars…**.

## Privacy

The plugin talks to Google's Calendar API from the machine that runs the plugins, with read-only access; it never changes your calendars. The Google token stays in the plugin's state folder on that machine. The events of the last week and next six weeks are stored on your host or server as the plugin's state (descriptions cut to 300 characters), for the apps to show. For prep notes, the event's title, time and place, and the notes that match its title, go to the AI you set in **Settings › AI**.
