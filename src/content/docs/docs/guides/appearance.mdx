**Settings › Appearance** changes how AIDE looks on this device, with a live preview of the editor
at the top of the page. **Settings › Language** picks the language of the app and of what plugins
write for you.

## Theme

| Setting | Choices | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Appearance** | **System**, **Light**, **Dark** | System |
| **Editor theme** | **System**, **Paper**, **Sepia**, **Night**, **Nord**, **Forest** | System |
| **Accent** | blue, indigo, purple, pink, red, orange, green, teal, graphite | blue |
| **Color headings with the accent** | On or off | Off |

**Appearance** sets light or dark for the whole app; **System** follows your device. The editor
theme colours notes: **System** follows light and dark, **Paper** and **Sepia** are light themes,
and **Night**, **Nord** and **Forest** are dark ones. The accent is AIDE's highlight colour, in the
editor and around the app.

## Text

| Setting | Choices | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Font** | **System**, **Serif**, **Rounded**, **Mono** | System |
| **Size** | 13 to 24 | 17 |
| **Line spacing** | **Compact**, **Normal**, **Relaxed** | Normal |
| **Large headings** | On or off | On |

These apply to the editor and the reader on this device.

## Wallpaper

A wallpaper shows behind the whole app, dimmed so text stays readable. Pick one image or a folder of
images; with a folder, AIDE shows a random one.

- **Mac and Linux:** **Image or folder** picks a file or a folder. **Include subfolders** takes
  images from folders inside it too.
- **iPhone:** **Choose photos** picks images from Photos into AIDE's own folder.
- **Rotate** shows another image of the folder on a timer: **Every** 1, 5, 15, 30 or 60 minutes, 3
  or 6 hours, or a day (on Linux, **Interval** in minutes).
- **Dim** (**Tint** on Linux) sets how much of the window colour covers the image.
- **Image opacity** <Badge text="Next release" /> sets how strongly the image itself shows, over the
  window's own colour. It is 100 % until you lower it.
- **Next wallpaper** shows another one now (on Linux beside **Shown now**, the image on screen).

## Window (Mac and Linux)

Under **Settings › Appearance › Window** on the Mac, or **Settings › General › Window** on Linux:

- **Panel side**: the left panel (Scratch, Wiki, …) on the **Left** or **Right**.
- **Show panel**: whether the panel shows.
- **Open notes in a new tab** (off by default): a note clicked in a list, the calendar or the graph
  opens in a new tab. Without it, hold <kbd>⌘</kbd> (Mac) or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> (Linux), or use the
  middle mouse button, to do the same.

## Window opacity (Mac and Linux) <Badge text="Next release" />

**Window opacity**, under **Settings › Appearance › Window** on the Mac and in the **Window** group
of **Settings › Appearance** on Linux, lets what's behind AIDE show through its windows, as copad's
`[window] opacity` did. It goes from 30 % to 100 % (opaque, the default), in 5 % steps, and every
window follows at once.

- The window's background, the panels and the main card become see-through. Text, icons and the
  terminal's text stay solid, and a note's page takes the card's colour, so it stays readable.
- Sheets, menus, popovers and Settings stay opaque.
- With a wallpaper, the image and its dim lie over the see-through window.
- **Mac:** below 100 %, **Blur behind the window** blurs what's behind it. While **Reduce
  transparency** is on (System Settings › Accessibility › Display), windows stay opaque and keep
  the value for later.
- **Linux:** the compositor blurs, when it does. The setting is also `opacity` under `[window]` in
  `config.toml`. While high contrast is on, windows stay opaque and keep the value for later.

The iPhone and iPad have no window opacity: their windows can't show what's behind them.

## Language

AIDE speaks English and Korean (한국어). There are two settings, under **Settings › Language** on
the Mac and iPhone and **Settings › General › Language** on Linux:

- **App language**: **System** (follows the device's language), **English** or **한국어**. The app
  switches at its next start.
- **Plugins write in**: the language plugins use for what they write into your notes and send you,
  such as briefings, recaps and Radar. It belongs to your account, so it is the same on every
  device, and plugins don't rewrite what they wrote before. Until you choose, it is English.

The assistant answers in the language you write to it. Voice input has its own **Language** under
**Settings › Assistant › Voice** (see [The assistant](/docs/guides/assistant/#voice)).
