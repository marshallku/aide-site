When Claude Code, Codex or Grok works on a task with more than one step, it writes its plan into your notes as a card: a short title, its steps, and a line that says what it's doing now. You see the work in your notes without reading the agent's terminal, and you can tick a step yourself at any time.

## What a card looks like

Cards are todos in the `board` folder, one note per project: an agent working in the `aide` repository writes to `board/aide.md`.

```markdown
- [/] Agent board plan [project:: aide] [agent:: claude] [card:: k7f2q] ➕ 2026-11-10
    > Codex review, round 2
    - [x] Survey Todos, editor, agents
    - [/] Codex plan review
    - [ ] Send to the owner
```

- The card's status is the agent's: `[ ]` to do, `[/]` doing, `[x]` done, `[-]` cancelled. A card waiting for you is open and tagged with your waiting tag (`#waiting`), with the agent's question on its status line.
- Its steps are the todos right under it. A step the agent drops from its plan before starting it is cancelled, never deleted. A step that was started or finished, by you or the agent, stays as it is.
- The agent can mark the card done only once no step under it is open: it finishes them, or cancels what's left on purpose. If you tick the card done yourself while steps are still open, they stay open.
- <Badge text="Next release" /> When the work lands, the agent adds what landed under the card: a commit or a URL such as a pull request, each on a `- ref:: …` line. A finished card says which pull request or commit closed it, and Details on the Board lists them as links.
- The fields and the rules for cards (only the `board` folder counts, a card's id is unique, a card always gets its completion date and never repeats) are in [Agent cards](/docs/guides/todos/#agent-cards).

You can edit the note as usual. An agent's change is always made on the note as it is now, so a tick you made a moment ago is kept.

## How agents learn it

The `aide-board` skill tells an agent when to add a card, how to keep its steps and status line current, when to move it to waiting, and how to finish it with a one-line outcome. On the Mac and Linux, AIDE gives it to your coding agents with the other [plugin skills](/docs/guides/terminal/#plugin-skills) once **Give plugins' skills to your coding agents** is on.

<Badge text="Next release" /> In an AIDE terminal pane, the card is linked to the agent's pane, and its link `aide://card/<vault>/<card>` opens that pane (see [Cards linked to panes](/docs/guides/terminal/#cards-linked-to-panes)). The agents use `aidectl board`; the [aidectl reference](/docs/developers/aidectl/#board) lists its commands. <Badge text="Next release" /> In a pane they need no flag or token: `aidectl` uses the pane's window's notes, and read-only agents can still use the board. Run `aidectl board list` to see the open cards yourself, or `aidectl board show <card>` for one card and its steps.

## Where you see it

<Badge text="Next release" /> Cards show on **Todos › Board**, in columns with a lane per project, each with what its agent's terminal pane is doing right now: see [The Board](/docs/guides/todos/#the-board). Agents' work stays off **Today** and **All**. A card's link opens its pane when one is open in this window, else the board with the card selected. When the board is off, or the card isn't on it (one finished long ago, say), the link opens the card's note instead.

## Giving work to an agent

<Badge text="Next release" /> To hand one of your todos to a coding agent, choose **Give to an agent** in its menu in **Today** or **All**, or in a card's menu on the Board. It becomes a card in its project's board note with the agent `agent`, in the column its status gives it: **To do** for a todo you haven't started. Ask an agent in a terminal pane to pick up the work you handed over: with the skill it finds those cards with `aidectl board list`, links its pane with `aidectl board link` and moves the card to **Doing** as it starts. An agent you ask to take one of your todos directly can make it a card itself with `aidectl board adopt`. You move cards yourself too: see [The Board](/docs/guides/todos/#the-board).

## Turning it off

The board is on. <Badge text="Next release" /> Turn it off with **Settings › Todos › Agent board**. While the board is off, an agent's changes to cards are refused, AIDE doesn't give agents the skill, there's no **Board** in **Todos**, and agents' todos show in **All** like any other; reading cards still works.
