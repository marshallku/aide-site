AIDE has a handful of ideas that every screen builds on. Knowing them makes the rest of the manual
quicker to read.

## The pieces

```text
  iPhone · Mac · Linux apps          aidectl · Claude (MCP)
             │                               │
             │ paired devices                │ this computer
             ▼                               ▼
  ┌──────────────────────── AIDE server ────────────────────────┐
  │  notes · todos · Organize · Feed + triage · AI · push       │
  │                                                             │
  │  database: the event log, settings, note metadata           │
  └───────┬──────────────────────────────┬──────────────────────┘
          │                              │ scoped tokens
          ▼                              ▼
  your vault: Markdown files     plugin runner: briefing, recap,
  in a folder                    Google Calendar, Slack, Radar…
```

In local mode there is no server box: the app opens the vault folder itself, and `aidectl` reaches it
through the running app. With a desktop host, the server, its database and the plugin runner run on
your computer as part of the app. [Ways to run AIDE](/docs/getting-started/ways-to-run/) compares
the three setups.

## Vaults and notes

A **vault** is a folder of Markdown files. Every `.md` file is a note, images and other files go in
`attachments/`, and AIDE keeps its own bookkeeping in a hidden `.aide/` folder. There's no export
step: the files are your notes, and other editors such as Obsidian can open the same folder. AIDE
picks up their edits and never overwrites them.

Notes link to each other with `[[wikilinks]]`, embed images and drawings, and can carry frontmatter
properties. Deleting a note moves it to **Settings › Recently deleted**; nothing is deleted for good.
See [Notes and the editor](/docs/guides/editor/) and [Vaults and your files](/docs/guides/vaults/).

## Scratch, then the wiki

Notes live in two places:

- **Scratch** is where you write without deciding where something goes. On the desktop, typing into
  an empty tab makes a scratch note.
- **Wiki** is your organized notes: topics, projects, and the periodic notes (daily, weekly, monthly,
  quarterly, yearly).

**Organize** moves scratch into the wiki. You pick scratch notes and choose **Propose**; AIDE suggests
a home for each, a new page or a section appended to an existing one. Nothing changes until you check
the proposals and **Apply** them, and applied notes are archived from Scratch, not deleted. See
[Scratch and Organize](/docs/guides/scratch/).

## Todos live in notes

There's no separate todo database. Every `- [ ]` line in any note is a todo, and the **Todos** screen
gathers them from the whole vault. A todo can carry dates, a priority, a time block, tags and an
estimate in Obsidian Tasks or Dataview syntax:

```markdown
- [ ] Send the invoice 📅 2026-10-24 ⏫ #work
- [/] 09:00 - 10:30 Write the quarterly plan [estimate:: 90m]
```

Checking one off in the Todos screen edits the line in its note. See [Todos](/docs/guides/todos/).

## The Feed

The **Feed** is one event log for everything that happens outside your notes. Plugins, scripts and
other services publish events to topics; your devices read the log and keep their place in it. Each
event is **triaged** into a bucket:

| Bucket | What goes there | Notifies |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Act on** | You have to do something: a deadline, an application, a payment | Always |
| **Need to know** | Important even with nothing to do | Unless you turn it off |
| **Might interest you** | Matches the topics of your own wiki | No |
| **Everything else** | The rest, collapsed | No |

The Feed needs a server or a desktop host. See [The Feed](/docs/guides/feed/) and
[Publish events to the Feed](/docs/developers/events/).

## Plugins

Plugins come in two kinds:

- **Server plugins** run beside the server in AIDE's plugin runner, each with its own limited token.
  They work while your devices are closed: the morning briefing, the evening recap, Google Calendar,
  Gmail, Slack, Radar and others. They are listed under **Settings › Plugins › On the server** and
  are off until you turn them on. They need a desktop host or your own server.
- **Device plugins** run inside the app, in a sandbox, with only the access you approve when you
  install them: a Pomodoro timer, Excalidraw drawings, a word count and others. They work in local
  mode too.

Plugins write into your notes through **slots**: a line such as `{{slot todo-carry.open}}` in a
template becomes a block that plugin keeps up to date. See [How plugins work](/docs/plugins/overview/).

## The assistant

The **Assistant** plans your day with you in a chat. It sees your open todos, today's calendar and
your free time, and answers with proposals (schedule this, estimate that, move it to tomorrow). Each
proposal waits for you to take or drop it; taken ones are edited into your notes with a record of
what changed. See [The assistant](/docs/guides/assistant/).

## Where AI is used

AI is optional everywhere. You choose where the model runs:

- **On your computer:** Claude Code or Codex, if you have them installed (Mac and Linux). They run with
  no tools, in an empty temporary folder: the prompt goes in and the reply comes out.
- **With your own Anthropic API key**, on the device or stored encrypted on your server.
- **On your server:** **Settings › AI** picks **Rules (no AI)**, **Anthropic API key** or
  **My gateway** (a gateway you run with your own Claude login).

Model output is never trusted as is: a reply that doesn't match what AIDE asked for is refused, and
changes to your notes wait for your approval.

| Feature | With AI | Without AI |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Organize | Proposes a home for each scratch note from what it says and your wiki's page names | By rules: a note's first `#tag` goes to `topics/<tag>`, notes with todos go to today's daily note, the rest to `inbox` |
| Feed triage | The model buckets each event, with a reason | By rules: deadline words ("due", "deadline", "apply by"…) mean Act on, events their publisher marks high severity mean Need to know, events that mention your wiki's topics mean Might interest you |
| The assistant | Plans with you | Not available |
| Meetings | Summary, decisions and action items | Recording and transcription still work; transcription runs on the device |
| Briefing, recap, catch-up and other AI plugins | Write their notes | Not available |

Everything else (the editor, todos, search, the graph, review, calendars, device plugins) never uses
AI. See [AI and models](/docs/guides/ai/).
